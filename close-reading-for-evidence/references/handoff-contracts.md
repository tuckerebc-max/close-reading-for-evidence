# Close Reading handoffs

## To Writing — `HOF-CR-WR-001`

Pass source IDs, evidence IDs, exact locators, claim status, uncertainty IDs, provenance IDs, and learner decisions. Transform a located observation or qualified interpretation into an evidence candidate. Writing must confirm audience, purpose, claim scope, and evidence promotion.

## To Argumentation — `HOF-CR-ARG-001`

Pass located evidence, source claim, observed reasoning, alternative interpretations, source IDs, evidence IDs, and uncertainty IDs. Argumentation may audit the source argument but must not treat analysis as endorsement.

## Return to QUEST

Route missing historical context, corpus gaps, source conflict requiring new research, or an unresolved research question to QUEST with the original evidence and uncertainty records intact.
